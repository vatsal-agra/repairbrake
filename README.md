# RepairBrake

The quoted amount is the only money PayPal is allowed to capture.

A later bill (voice or photo) is split into consented lines and extras. Consented lines capture. Upsells stay authorized and uncaptured until the customer taps yes.

PayPal AI Hackathon entry. See `DEVELOPMENT.md` for the build gates.

## Status

Phase 1: quote/consent math + PayPal capture client (unit-tested). Live sandbox partial-capture spike is blocked until sandbox credentials are in `.env` (never committed).
